About Me

I am a research assistant in Prof. Jun Li’s research group at Tsinghua University, working on action-oriented world models and autonomous driving planning.

I received my bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from Guangzhou Institute of Science and Technology in June 2026. Previously, I was a jointly trained student at the School of Vehicle and Mobility, Tsinghua University, a visiting student at YU Vision Group, Great Bay University, and an embodied intelligence algorithm engineer at the Center for Brain Inspired Computing Research (CBICR), Tsinghua University.

Research Interests

My research focuses on learning and reasoning about the real world with world models. I am particularly interested in:

  • Real-world dynamics and interactions. Understanding how the world evolves and how agents interact, beyond predicting the next observation.
  • Task-relevant latent representations. Learning features that matter for downstream goals while reducing the modeling of irrelevant changes.

I explore these questions through world models, embodied intelligence, and autonomous driving.

Publications & Research

Auto-JEPA: A Latent World Model of Continuous Intent for End-to-End Autonomous Driving

Jiwei Yang (First author)

Under review at AAAI 2027.

Learning continuous future driving intent through joint-embedding prediction. Auto-JEPA achieves 91.3 PDMS on NAVSIM v1 and 89.1 EPDMS on NAVSIM v2. Semantic occlusion experiments probe its sensitivity to traffic participants that affect future driving.

arXivPDFCode

SDRA: Spatiotemporally Decoupled VLM-Enhanced Reinforcement Learning for Energy-Efficient Heavy-Vehicle Driving

Jiwei Yang (First author)

Under review at AAAI 2027.

A two-level framework separates lane-level decisions from cruise–coast control, using a vision-language model for scene understanding. Optimized trajectories reduce fuel consumption by 6.7% in experiments using real heavy-vehicle data.

OP-RTDETR: Real-Time Outdoor Parking Space Detection with Grouped Convolutions and Group-Level Contrastive Denoising

Jiwei Yang (First author)

SCI Q2 journal · Major revision.

An RT-DETR-based detector with a GC-VanillaNet backbone, group-level contrastive denoising, and grouped one-to-many matching, evaluated for detection accuracy and inference speed on PKLot.

CHyFN: A Consistency-Aware Hybrid Feature Network for Robust Object Recognition in Complex Scenes

Jiwei Yang (First author)

Combining global structure and local texture across diffusion timesteps with contextual consistency analysis. Experiments cover camouflaged object detection, fine-grained classification, and generated-image detection.
